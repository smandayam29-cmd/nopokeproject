The problem

Scared of shots? You're not alone.

According to a study by Jennifer McLenon and Mary Rogers, about 20–50% of adolescents have a fear of needles. This is a major problem, as teens are missing out on crucial medicine they need to stay healthy.

So I came up with a solution. It is called NoPoke. Kids will find it fun to pronounce, and adults will know it easily.

How it works

A spring and a jet stream

1. Load it

The hospital inserts the medicine. That's all they have to do.

2. Spring

A spring pushes the medicine out as a super-thin, super-fast jet stream.

3. Done

The stream goes through the skin. No needle, no poke.

Want NoPoke? Just ask your doctor. Either you pay out of pocket, or your insurance pays. Expected price: about $10–15.

Why NoPoke

Families get to decide.

Other brands

Brands like PharmaJet are marketed towards hospitals and are rarely available to the public. Most families have never heard of them.

NoPoke

I market towards parents and kids so they can ask their doctor for NoPoke. That makes families the decision-makers and hospitals the givers of the vaccines.

Why now

The world is ready.

12.7%

Economically: according to the National Law Review, the needle-less injector market is growing 12.7% a year!

No sharps

Socially: families want shots that are less scary, and humanitarian groups want needle-free options because there are no used needles to throw away.

How it's made

NoPoke is made from medical-grade plastic, with the nozzles, springs, housing, and safety mechanisms made by local manufacturers and built to meet FDA safety rules. NoPoke is not FDA cleared yet.
